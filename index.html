Our Sacred Mission

Under the sacred mandate and unbroken line of Apostolic Succession of His Eminence, Archbishop Ronald Saunders (Spain), the ACC Southeast Asia Mission is officially established to shepherd faithful communities, conduct holy prayers, and spread Christ's compassionate love across the region.

Managed by Lay-Reader Joseph Tu Quoc Huy, our office is deeply dedicated to fostering spiritual growth, theological depth, and independent, self-supporting ministries serving Vietnam and neighboring nations in Southeast Asia. We embrace all souls with pastoral zeal, bringing traditional faith to modern lives.

Authorized Regional Ministries
🕁 Holy Baptism Administering the holy sacrament of new life and welcoming souls into Christ's family.
🕁 Liturgical Prayers Conducting official public Morning and Evening prayers to strengthen daily faith.
🕁 Bible Study Guides Nurturing the emerging local communities through rigorous and faithful scripture studies.
🕁 Pastoral Care & Funerals Providing spiritual support, visiting the sick, and conducting respectful burial services.
