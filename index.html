Our Sacred Mission

Under the sacred mandate and Apostolic Succession of His Eminence, Archbishop Ronald Saunders (Spain), the ACC Southeast Asia Mission is officially established to shepherd faithful communities, conduct holy prayers, and spread Christ's compassionate love across the region.

Managed by Lay-Reader Joseph Tu Quoc Huy, we are dedicated to fostering spiritual growth, theological depth, and independent, self-supporting ministries serving Vietnam and neighboring nations in Southeast Asia.

Authorized Ministries
🕁 Holy Baptism
Administering the sacrament of new life for souls.
🕁 Liturgical Prayers
Conducting official Morning and Evening public prayers.
🕁 Bible Study Guides
Nurturing faithful communities through Holy Scripture.
🕁 Pastoral Care & Funerals
Visiting the sick and conducting respectful burial services.
