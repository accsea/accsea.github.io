Our Sacred Mission

Under the sacred mandate and unbroken line of Apostolic Succession of His Eminence, Archbishop Ronald Saunders (Spain), the ACC Southeast Asia Mission is officially established to shepherd faithful communities, conduct holy prayers, and spread Christ's compassionate love across the region.

Managed by Lay-Reader Joseph Quoc Huy, our office is deeply dedicated to fostering spiritual growth, theological depth, and independent, self-supporting ministries serving Vietnam and neighboring nations in Southeast Asia. We embrace all souls with pastoral zeal, bringing traditional faith to modern lives.
